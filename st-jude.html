Through JD Burch for St. Jude, I join the Relay community each year to raise money for St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital. I started in 2022 with lawn-mowing incentives. Each year brings its own approach; in 2025 and 2026, the theme was Marching for St. Jude, sharing my Michigan Marching Band experience with supporters.

Campaign complete / 2026

Five years of showing up.

JD Burch for St. Jude 2026, themed Marching for St. Jude, has ended, raising $3,100.12 against a $3,000 goal. Thank you to everyone who donated, shared the campaign, and followed along with my Michigan Marching Band experience.
