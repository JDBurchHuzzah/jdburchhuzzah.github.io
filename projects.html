JD Burch for St. Jude

Through JD Burch for St. Jude, I run annual fundraising campaigns supporting St. Jude Children’s Research Hospital. I organize the campaigns and create donation incentives. The 2025 and 2026 campaigns used the theme Marching for St. Jude, sharing my Michigan Marching Band experience with supporters.

Impact: $10,191.77 raised across five completed campaigns from 2022–2026. The 2026 campaign raised $3,100.12 against a $3,000 goal.
