## Music at Michigan

I play tenor sax in the Michigan Marching Band, Basketball Band, and University Band. Music is a big part of my life, and sharing my experience in the MMB has become part of how I fundraise, too.

## Giving back

### JD Burch for St. Jude

Through my annual St. Jude fundraiser, I raise money alongside the Relay community. I started in 2022 with lawn-mowing incentives. In 2025 and 2026, the theme was **Marching for St. Jude**, connecting donations with my time rehearsing and performing with the Michigan Marching Band.

Together, supporters raised **$10,191.77 across the 2022–2026 campaigns**. [Explore the campaigns and results]({{ '/st-jude/' | relative_url }})

### Trail to Eagle

I first attended Trail to Eagle (TTE) with Blue Troop in 2019 and later served as a Junior Assistant Scoutmaster. Today, I return each summer as a **Scoutmaster and Health Officer**, helping give other Scouts the kind of camp experience that meant so much to me.

[TTE](https://michiganscouting.org/camping/trail-to-eagle/) is a week-long specialty camp where Scouts attend without their home troops and work toward the merit badges needed for Eagle Scout. I lead a provisional troop, bringing Scouts from different communities together for the week. I also serve as a primary member of the medical staff, putting my Red Cross training and certifications to work supporting campers’ health and safety.
